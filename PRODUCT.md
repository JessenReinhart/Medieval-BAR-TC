# PRODUCT.md — Medieval-BAR-TC Main Menu

## Register
product

## Target Users & Context
- **Audience:** Real-time strategy players (Age of Empires II DE, 0 A.D., Total War, and BAR veterans) who expect a cohesive historical medieval setting.
- **Context:** Booting the game from desktop or lobby, configuring custom skirmish matches against AI commanders, adjusting graphics/audio parameters, reviewing game credits/licenses, or pausing an active match to change settings.
- **Primary Goal:** Seamlessly launch or resume historical RTS battles without friction, with clear tactical feedback on map choice, victory conditions, team distribution, and difficulty.

## Product Purpose
The in-engine shell menu and match launcher for **Medieval-BAR-TC**, replacing raw engine defaults with an authentic historical interface. It controls match configuration, startscript compilation, engine reloading, live sound/graphics preferences, and documentation.

## Brand Personality & Aesthetic
- **Tone:** Grounded, authentic historical RTS inspired by 0 A.D. and medieval cartography. Heavy dark iron, chiselled stone framing, burnished bronze fasteners, and rich heraldic highlights.
- **Atmosphere:** A campaign war-room table by candlelight — dark slate background with deep earth undertones, crisp parchment labels with high contrast, and warm amber/bronze interactive states.
- **Materials:** Burnished bronze borders, deep slate/iron backing plates, warm amber active triggers, and crisp ivory typographic ink.

## Anti-References & Bans
- **No AI Cream/Parchemnt Slop:** No saturated beige/sand/paper body washes that wash out contrast or feel like generic AI templates. The surface is deep iron and dark slate.
- **No Neon/Glass SaaS Look:** No glassmorphism blurs, floating pastel cards, purple gradients, or tech-product tropes.
- **No Tracked Eyebrow Spam:** No tiny all-caps spaced labels above every heading.
- **No Hero-Metric Clichés:** No big meaningless stat numbers on cards.
- **No Unresponsive Buttons:** Every interactive control must provide distinct hover, active, selected, and disabled visual states.

## Accessibility & Quality Floor
- **WCAG AA Contrast:** All readable labels and data hit ≥ 4.5:1 contrast against their backing plates.
- **State Completeness:** Default, hover, pressed/active, selected, and disabled states across all buttons, segmented selectors, and toggles.
- **Keyboard Ergonomics:** Escape toggles or navigates backward; swallows raw game inputs while open.
- **Resolution Agnostic:** Operates crisply from 1280x720 up to 4K without text clipping or button overlapping.
